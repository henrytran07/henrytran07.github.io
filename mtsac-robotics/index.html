ABOUT THE TEAM

Mt.SAC Robotics is an internationally competing collegiate robotics team dedicated to providing students with hands-on engineering experience.

My role on the team

I served as the lead programmer of the team, designing and optimizing autonomous routines for competitions where the robot had 30 seconds to score as many points as possible. I also led the programming skills challenge, where our robot achieved 74 points, the highest score in Mt.SAC Robotics Team history and the #1 autonomous skills score in California at the 2026 World Championship.

World Skills Run

Conclusion

The experience exceeded my expectations and became one of the steepest learning curves I have ever experienced. There were days where I spent 12-15 hours in the room fine-tuning autonomous routines, fixing issues, and so on. This journey became the foundation that inspired me to continue pursuing engineering opportunities through future teams at UCLA.
