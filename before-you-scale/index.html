Before You Scale

The Cost of Supervision Mismatch in PRM Distillation

  • Saksham Kapoor

I started this project in the summer of 2025, exploring whether a smaller model could be taught by a larger one to give feedback as effectively as a pair of larger models. The work paused for a stretch and picked back up in June 2026, and the resulting paper was accepted to the COLM 2026 ER Workshop in San Francisco. What drew me in was a finding in the prior work we built on: feedback from two models tailors the response better for the student model. What we found was the opposite of what we expected. The teacher did benefit from the extra information, but the student never inherited that richness in our setup.
